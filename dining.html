Global Culinary Journey

A Taste of the World

Our executive chefs bring the absolute best of global cuisine directly to your table. Explore our authentic, gourmet signature dishes from around the world.

🇮🇹 Italy

White Truffle Tagliatelle

Hand-rolled pasta tossed in a rich Parmigiano-Reggiano cream, finished with freshly shaved Alba white truffles.

🇯🇵 Japan

Premium Omakase Boat

An exquisite selection of Bluefin Toro, Uni, and Hokkaido scallops, masterfully sliced by our resident sushi masters.

🇫🇷 France

Duck Confit & Foie Gras

Slow-cooked duck leg with a perfect crispy skin, paired with pan-seared foie gras and a tart cherry reduction.

🇮🇳 India

The Royal Thali

A lavish spread of rich Murgh Makhani (Butter Chicken), slow-cooked Dal Makhani, saffron rice, and garlic butter naan.

🇲🇽 Mexico

Wagyu Beef Birria Tacos

Melt-in-your-mouth Wagyu beef slow-braised in ancho chilies, served in artisanal corn tortillas with a rich consommé dip.

🇱🇧 Lebanon

Grand Mezze Platter

A luxurious array of smoked Baba Ganoush, velvety Hummus, fresh Tabbouleh, and warm, wood-fired pita bread.

🇹🇭 Thailand

Tiger Prawn Tom Yum

A perfectly balanced aromatic, spicy, and sour broth featuring jumbo tiger prawns, lemongrass, and fresh galangal.

🇺🇸 USA

Dry-Aged Tomahawk

A stunning 45-day dry-aged bone-in Ribeye, wood-fire grilled to perfection and served with black garlic butter.

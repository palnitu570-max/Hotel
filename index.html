Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about your stay at Luxe Hotel. If you need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact our 24/7 concierge service.

Yes, we offer luxury chauffeur service to and from the international airport. Please provide your flight details to our concierge at least 24 hours in advance to arrange pick-up.
